What Is Eid?

Eid is a magical and holy celebration observed by Muslims all over the world. There are two major Eids: Eid al-Fitr (celebrating the completion of Ramadan) and Eid al-Adha (the festival of sacrifice).

It's a wonderful day filled with morning prayers, wearing beautiful new clothes, eating sweet delicacies, giving charity, and sharing gifts (Eidi) with loved ones! 💕
