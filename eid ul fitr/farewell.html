Chapter I — Farewell, Ramadan
وَدَاعاً رَمَضَان
Thirty nights of devotion, of stillness, of prayer.
The crescent that summoned you now sets you free.
Another year must pass before we meet again —
Ya Ramadan, the ummah weeps your parting.
